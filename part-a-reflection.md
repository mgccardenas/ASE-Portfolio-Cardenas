Reflection for Part A:

After learning about Agentic Software Engineering, I realized that developing AI-powered systems involves more than just making applications that can perform tasks automatically. Although AI agents can make work faster and more efficient, developers must also consider the possible risks and consequences of allowing them to make decisions with less human intervention. I learned that using Agile methods is helpful because it allows developers to continuously test, improve, and adjust their systems based on feedback and actual results.

One thing that stood out to me is the importance of human oversight. AI agents may be capable of analyzing information and making decisions, but they are not always correct and can still produce unexpected results. As future Computer Engineers, we should not only focus on creating innovative technologies but also make sure that they are safe, reliable, and respectful of people's privacy. For me, responsible AI development means finding a balance between allowing AI to work independently and knowing when human intervention is necessary. Technology should make people's lives easier without putting their safety, privacy, and well-being at risk.
